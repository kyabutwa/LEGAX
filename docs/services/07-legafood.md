# LegaX — LegaFood

## LegaFood — Food Service & Delivery Coordination

**Canonical definition**

LegaFood is the LegaX service responsible for coordinating governed food-related discovery, ordering, preparation, fulfillment, delivery, pickup, service relationships, and associated economic and operational events among customers, food providers, workers, couriers, communities, and facilities, subject to applicable food-safety and legal requirements.

LegaFood represents menus or offerings, availability, orders, preparation states, fulfillment, delivery or pickup, substitutions, cancellations, refunds, and evidence without treating a provider listing as universal proof of safety, inventory, or legal compliance.

**Canonical flow:** Discovery → Selection → Order Intent → Authorization/Payment → Provider Acceptance → Preparation → Fulfillment → Delivery/Pickup → Completion → Event/Evidence.

**Core boundaries:** menu representation is not guaranteed availability; order acceptance is not preparation completion; preparation completion is not delivery; delivery is not payment settlement; provider compliance assertions retain provenance.

**Safety and privacy:** food-service policies, provider participation, relevant safety evidence, delivery constraints, allergies or dietary information where intentionally provided, and incident handling must be purpose-bound and protected.

**AI boundary:** AI may recommend meals, optimize preparation/delivery, detect operational anomalies, and assist customer support, but cannot invent safety verification or authorize consequential actions outside policy.

**Composition:** LegaFood may use LegaPay, LegaRide, LegaBooking, LegaAds, LegaAccess, and LegaWork through explicit contracts.

**Status:** Foundational service contract — implementation intentionally deferred.
