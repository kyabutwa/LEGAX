# LegaX — LegaHealth

## LegaHealth — Health-Service Coordination

**Canonical definition**

LegaHealth is the LegaX service responsible for coordinating governed relationships between participating people, health-service providers, facilities, appointments, resources, care-related workflows, payments where applicable, and supporting evidence, while preserving clinical authority, professional responsibility, privacy, safety, and applicable legal requirements outside the LegaX platform's own identity and access foundations.

LegaHealth is not itself a universal medical authority and must not represent an AI recommendation, provider listing, identity verification, or administrative status as clinical truth or professional judgment.

**Canonical flow:** Service Need/Request → Identity/Eligibility → Provider/Appointment Context → Authorization → Service Encounter/Coordination → Outcome/Record Reference → Event/Evidence.

**Core boundaries:** identity is not medical qualification; provider participation is not clinical authorization; an appointment is not treatment; an AI output is not diagnosis or clinical authority; payment is not proof of care; an external clinical record remains governed by its authoritative source and integration contract.

**Privacy and safety:** health information requires strong purpose limitation, minimization, access control, provenance, retention, consent/legal-basis handling where applicable, incident controls, and jurisdiction-specific governance. High-impact operations require appropriately qualified human authority.

**AI boundary:** intelligence may assist navigation, summarization, matching, administrative coordination, anomaly detection, and decision support where legally and clinically appropriate, but must not silently replace authorized clinical judgment or governance.

**Status:** Foundational service contract — implementation intentionally deferred.
