# LegaX — LegaAds

## LegaAds — Governed Advertising & Discovery

**Canonical definition**

LegaAds is the LegaX service responsible for coordinating governed advertising relationships among advertisers, providers, communities, publishers or service surfaces, and participating audiences, including campaigns, eligibility, placement, delivery, measurement, billing, and evidence while preserving participant agency, privacy, purpose limitation, and separation between advertising influence and authorization.

LegaAds must not turn LegaX identity, access history, health information, financial information, precise location, or other sensitive information into unrestricted surveillance or automatic commercial profiling. Data use must be governed by explicit purpose, policy, lawful basis where applicable, minimization, retention, and participant/community controls.

**Canonical flow:** Campaign → Eligibility/Policy → Audience/Context Selection → Authorized Placement → Delivery → Measurement → Billing → Event/Evidence.

**Core boundaries:** audience eligibility is not permission to expose identity; an impression is not engagement; engagement is not conversion; conversion is not payment settlement; targeting signals are not authority; recommendation is not consent.

**AI boundary:** AI may optimize placement, match relevant offers, detect fraud, forecast performance, and assist campaign operations, but must operate within privacy and governance constraints and cannot manufacture consent or authority.

**Status:** Foundational service contract — implementation intentionally deferred.
