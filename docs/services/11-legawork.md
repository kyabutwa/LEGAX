# LegaX — LegaWork

## LegaWork — Professional & Work Coordination

**Canonical definition**

LegaWork is the LegaX service responsible for coordinating governed professional relationships and work activity among workers, freelancers, clients, organizations, providers, and opportunities, including professional identity, skills, evidence, matching, proposals, contracts, projects, milestones, deliverables, reviews, payments, disputes, and work history.

LegaWork treats professional capability as evidence-based and lifecycle-managed. A declared skill, verified qualification, demonstrated capability, completed work, observed performance, and professional level are distinct states of information and must not be silently conflated.

**Canonical capability progression:** Declared Skill → Verification where applicable → Assessment → Demonstrated Capability → Experience/Completed Work → Evidence-backed Professional Level.

**Canonical work flow:** Opportunity → Matching → Proposal → Acceptance/Contract → Project → Milestones → Deliverables → Review/Acceptance → Payment/Settlement → Completion → Work Evidence.

**Core boundaries:** profile is not qualification; declared skill is not verified capability; verified credential is not proof of every practical skill; matching is not employment or contract; contract acceptance is not completed work; payment initiation is not settlement; review is evidence but not infallible truth.

A participant may legitimately act as both client and worker/freelancer in different or even related contexts, subject to conflicts, authority, policy, and contractual boundaries.

**AI boundary:** intelligence may match opportunities, identify skills, summarize work, forecast project risk, recommend candidates, or assist workflows, but cannot fabricate experience, grant professional authority, or independently create a consequential employment/contract/payment decision.

**Composition:** LegaWork may use LegaPay, LegaBooking, LegaAccess, LegaNetwork, LegaAward, and other services through explicit contracts.

**Status:** Foundational service contract — implementation intentionally deferred.
